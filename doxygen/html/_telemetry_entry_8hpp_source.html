15/**
16 * Interface for individual telemetry entries. Intended for use by backends;
17 * most users should use the TelemetryTable class.
18 *
19 * Implementations must not throw from logging, metadata, or discard-checking
20 * methods. Recoverable failures should be reported through
21 * TelemetryRegistry::ReportWarning() and skipped.
22 *
23 * Methods on the same entry may be called concurrently. Implementations must
24 * copy spans and string views they retain after a method returns.
25 */
