void wpi::RobotController::SetBrownoutVoltages ( wpi::units::volt_t brownoutVoltage,
wpi::units::volt_t recoveryVoltage )
static

Set the voltages where the robot will enter and recover from brownout.

The brownout voltage must be between 5 V and 8 V, inclusive. The recovery voltage must be no greater than 8.5 V and at least 0.5 V above the brownout voltage.

Parameters
brownoutVoltagethe voltage where the robot will enter brownout
recoveryVoltagethe voltage where the robot will recover from brownout
