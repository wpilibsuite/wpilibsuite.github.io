◆ flags

unsigned int wpi::nt::Event::flags {0}

Event flags (NT_EventFlags).

Also indicates the data included with the event:

  • NT_EVENT_CONNECTED or NT_EVENT_DISCONNECTED: GetConnectionInfo()
  • NT_EVENT_PUBLISH, NT_EVENT_UNPUBLISH, or NT_EVENT_PROPERTIES: GetTopicInfo()
  • NT_EVENT_VALUE, NT_EVENT_VALUE_LOCAL: GetValueData()
  • NT_EVENT_LOG_MESSAGE: GetLogMessage()
  • NT_EVENT_TIME_SYNC: GetTimeSyncEventData()
